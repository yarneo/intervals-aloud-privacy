Support

Intervals Aloud

Email ppyarden@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who built the app. Bug reports and feature requests are both welcome — say which iPhone and which iOS version, and it will be easier to fix.

I can't hear the cues

  • Check the silent switch on the side of the phone. Cues respect it.
  • Turn the volume up while a routine is running — that sets the volume for the cues rather than for the ringer.
  • In the routine's settings, check that Speak interval names is on.

Does it keep working with the screen off?

Yes. Cues carry on with the phone locked and in a pocket, and play over music from any app. There is a Live Activity on the Lock Screen showing the interval and the countdown.

Can I make my own routines?

Yes. Tap +, choose a shape, set the times and name it. You can also start from any built-in routine and change it, or nest repeating rounds inside a routine for circuits and HIIT.

The voices

Three recorded voices ship with the app, plus your phone's built-in voice. The recorded ones are bundled, not streamed, so they work offline and sound the same on every device. Interval names you invent that are not in the recorded vocabulary are spoken by the system voice.

Apple Watch

The watch app runs routines on their own and speaks the same cues. Your routines and settings sync from the phone automatically.

Apple Health

Finished routines can be saved to Health as workouts so they appear in Fitness and count toward your rings. This is optional, and the app only ever writes to Health — it never reads your health data.

Privacy
