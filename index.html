Privacy Policy

Intervals Aloud · last updated 1 September 2026

The short version

Intervals Aloud collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. It does not contain any third-party tracking or advertising code. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server to send it to.

What stays on your device

  • Your routines — their names, intervals, durations and settings.
  • Your history of finished routines.
  • Your preferences, such as which voice you chose and the volume.

These are stored on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, they also sync between your own Apple devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That sync happens inside your personal iCloud account. We cannot see it, and it is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Health data

If you grant permission, finished routines are written to Apple Health as workouts so they appear in Fitness. This is optional, and the app works fully without it.

The app only writes to Health. It never reads your health data. Health data never leaves your device by way of this app, and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Microphone, location, contacts

Not used. The app never requests access to them.

Network access

The app does not require an internet connection and makes no network requests of its own. The recorded voices are bundled with the app, not streamed.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. To remove workouts already written to Apple Health, use the Health app, which controls that data independently. To remove synced copies, sign out of or clear iCloud data for the app in your device's iCloud settings.
