Privacy Policy

Intervals Aloud · last updated 11 October 2026

The short version

Intervals Aloud has no account, advertising or tracking. Your personal workout library, edits and completion history stay on your devices and, if enabled, your own iCloud account. Optional online video import sends a video link to our service and may use Google Gemini to build a routine. Source routines can be reused across users. Custom voice generation sends the cue text and selected voice to our speech service.

What stays on your device

  • Your routines — their names, intervals, durations and settings.
  • Your history of finished routines.
  • Your preferences, such as which voice you chose and the volume.
  • Generated speech audio, cached so a prepared routine works offline.

Your history and generated audio stay on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, routine definitions may sync between your own Apple devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. Completion history and Health data are never placed in iCloud by Intervals Aloud. We cannot see anything in your personal iCloud account, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Health data

If you grant permission, finished routines are written to Apple Health as workouts so they appear in Fitness. This is optional, and the app works fully without it.

The app only writes to Health. It never reads your health data. Health data never leaves your device by way of this app, and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Microphone, location, contacts

Not used. The app never requests access to them.

Custom voice generation

The four coach voices include a bundled vocabulary. If a routine or interval name is not in that vocabulary, the app sends only the complete cue text and the selected voice to the Intervals Aloud speech service over HTTPS. The service runs Kokoro on Google Cloud in Israel, returns a WAV file, and does not save the cue text or audio. It has no account identifier and cannot see your full library, history, photos, videos, music or Health data.

The generated audio is cached on your device and may be copied to your paired Apple Watch. It is not generated again unless that local cache is removed. Google Cloud processes basic request information such as an IP address to deliver and protect the service, under Google Cloud's privacy notice.

Online video import

Online import is optional. Before the first online import, we ask your permission to send the link to our service and use Google Gemini when needed. You can withdraw this permission in Settings. Previously saved routines remain available.

Our service runs on Google Cloud. It first looks for an existing source routine and usable description or chapter information. If more analysis is needed, it sends the public YouTube URL to Google Gemini. For supported TikTok and Instagram posts, our service may retrieve temporary video clips and send them to Gemini to read the exercise names and timing. It does not use your platform login or your personal cookies. The temporary clips are deleted when the request finishes. Pictures and saved videos you import directly are read on your device; those files are not uploaded by online link import.

We store extracted source plans, their source links, source evidence and cache timestamps in Google Cloud Firestore. These plans may be returned to other users importing the same video. They are retained for up to 30 days after a successful refresh, plus any delay in automatic deletion, and may be refreshed or analyzed again. Your edits and chosen durations are not added to this shared source cache. When the source asks for a duration choice, your answer is sent to our service to assemble your draft for that request.

We use Firebase App Check and App Attest to verify that requests come from the app. Google processes app installation identifiers, attestation information and basic network information such as IP addresses to operate and protect the service. Our services also retain operational logs and usage totals for reliability and spending limits. These are not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps.

Gemini processing is subject to Google's Gemini API terms. Our analysis requests disable stored conversation history; this does not override Google's security and abuse monitoring retention. Google Cloud and Firebase processing is described in Google Cloud's privacy notice and Firebase's privacy information.

Offline use

Prepared routines, previously generated voice cues and the bundled coach vocabulary work without an internet connection. New online link imports and new custom voice generation require a connection.

Children

The app has no account, advertising or tracking and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Online import and custom cue text are handled as described above.

Deleting your data

To ask us to remove an imported source routine from our shared cache, contact us using the details below and include the source video link. Removing your app does not remove shared source routines requested by other users.

Deleting the app removes its on-device history and cached audio. To remove workouts already written to Apple Health, use the Health app, which controls that data independently. To remove synced routine definitions, clear the app's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.
